Not a Key

No login. No signal needed. Just a key.

Not a Key turns your iPhone into a Bluetooth key for your car. One press of the Action Button opens the boot, or closes it if it's already open.

Support

Something not working, or a question? Email sam@nevelen.com and include your iPhone model and what you were trying to do.

Common fixes

  • "Car not in range": stay within a few metres of the car with Bluetooth on.
  • "Car busy, try again": the car allows a limited number of Bluetooth connections. Close other car apps and try again.
  • "Unlock your iPhone once, then try again": after a restart, unlock your iPhone once so the key can be read.
  • New phone: remove the key in Settings and pair again with your key card.
