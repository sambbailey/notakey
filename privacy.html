Privacy Policy

Not a Key · Last updated 19 October 2026

Not a Key turns your iPhone into a Bluetooth key for your car. It was built to work without accounts, servers or the internet, so there is very little to say here: Not a Key does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, the app keeps a few things on your device only:

  • Your car key. A cryptographic key created on your iPhone and kept in the Secure Enclave. It cannot be exported, never syncs to iCloud and never leaves the device.
  • Your car's VIN, which you enter during setup so the app can find your car.
  • The last readings from your car, such as battery level, range, lock and climate state, so they can be shown when the car is out of range.
  • Your settings, such as whether Face ID is required before unlocking.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Bluetooth

Not a Key talks directly to your car over Bluetooth. Commands and readings pass only between your iPhone and your car. Nothing goes through the internet or any server.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID for unlocking, the check is done by iOS. The app only learns whether it succeeded and never has access to your face data.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-in. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not use third-party services that collect data.

Children

Not a Key is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
